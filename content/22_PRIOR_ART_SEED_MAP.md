# 22 — Prior-art Seed Map — không phải kết luận novelty cuối cùng

Mục này lưu những family/công trình đã được đưa vào đối chiếu trong origin dialogue. Nó không tuyên bố search exhaustiveness. Vòng Deep Audit phải kiểm chứng bibliographic data, đọc nguồn gốc và so claim-by-claim.

## D. M. Armstrong — *A Combinatorial Theory of Possibility* (1989)

**Overlap seed:** non-actual possibilities qua recombination của constituents.

**BL∞ relation cần audit:** `overlapsWith` BL-GR/combinatorial possibility; không mặc định identity với embedded-observer + reachability + provenance architecture.

## David Lewis — *On the Plurality of Worlds* (1986)

**Overlap seed:** modal realism, plurality of possible worlds, recombination.

**BL∞ relation cần audit:** relation với optional Plenitude extension, không tự map toàn OODP/BLOK.

## Alexius Meinong — Theory of Objects / object theory

**Overlap seed:** intentional objects, objects described/thought despite ordinary nonexistence; useful for representation/referent distinctions.

## David Chalmers — conceivability and possibility

**Overlap seed:** conditions under which conceivability may inform modal possibility.

**BL∞ relevance:** prevents crude inference `conceivable -> actual` without bridge.

## Max Tegmark — Mathematical Universe Hypothesis

**Overlap seed:** very broad ontology where mathematical existence is tied strongly to physical existence.

**BL∞ audit question:** is BL∞ reducible to mathematical-structure ontology or intentionally ontology-open beyond this commitment?

## David Deutsch / Chiara Marletto — Constructor Theory

**Overlap seed:** physical transformations characterized as possible/impossible, constructors, tasks.

**BL∞ relevance:** strongest prior family for constructibility and reachability aspects.

## Ruliad / Ruliology / computational universe families

**Overlap seed:** all-computation spaces, observers embedded inside computational totalities, accessibility/sampling constraints.

**BL∞ audit question:** does BL∞ contribute a distinct architecture through endogenous imagination targets, recursively expanded reachability, observation filter and research governance, or is a formal reduction possible?

## Assembly Theory

**Overlap seed:** histories/pathways for constructing objects; combinatorial possibility versus observed/selected structures.

**BL∞ relevance:** useful for separating combinatorial space from historically reached actual space.

## Quine–Duhem / underdetermination families

**Overlap seed:** observational data may underdetermine theory/global ontology.

**BL∞ relevance:** formal relatives of the bacterium thought experiment.

## Gödel / Turing

**Use with strict guardrails:** incompleteness/undecidability can support humility about formal systems but must not be abused to prove metaphysical plenitude.

## Clark & Chalmers / Hutchins

**Overlap seed:** extended/distributed cognition; cognition can include external artifacts, social/tool systems.

**BL∞ relevance:** human+AI cognitive supply chain and tool neutrality, without claiming every use of AI is automatically rigorous.

## Open science / metascience / DeSci / open peer review

**Overlap seed:** preservation, public review, provenance, versioning and alternative research governance.

**BL∞ novelty question:** whether OODP/PCRO/Level-Matched Critique/semantic indexing constitute a new integrated protocol or recombination of existing open-science mechanisms.

## Audit rule

For every source S, create a matrix:

| Claim ID BL∞ | Exact claim from S | Relation | Same assumptions? | Same consequence? | Prior to origin? | Independent derivation evidence? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

No novelty conclusion should be based only on keyword similarity.

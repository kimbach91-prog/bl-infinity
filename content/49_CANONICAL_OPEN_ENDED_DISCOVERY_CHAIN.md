Correction:


Added:

```text
BL-DGE = Dimension Genesis Engine

BL-OME = Ontology Mutation Engine
BL-CDE = Civilizational Discovery Ecology
```

The architecture now has a mechanism for detecting that its own representation may be too poor and for proposing new dimensions/primitive distinctions without lowering validation standards.

# 28. Preferred canonical proposition

> **BL∞ is an open-ended discovery system rather than a fixed ladder of Reality and hypothesis. Reality, GiaTai, mixed/contested states and Unknown occupy an extensible epistemic phase space whose dimensions are themselves revisable. The architecture remains open-ended because it can revise not only answers, but also its questions, categories, dimensions and languages.**

The adopted loop is:

```text

-> DECONSTRUCT
-> REGRESS
-> ALIGN
-> EXECUTE_OR_LOOP
```

### PLAY

### DECONSTRUCT
Separate rule purpose, constraint, authority, exception, ambiguity and unknown.

### REGRESS
Use a desirable future state as a boundary condition:

```text

```

This is backcasting/requirement regression, not backward-time information.

### ALIGN
Restate the rule interpretation and proposed route to the relevant authority where appropriate.

### EXECUTE OR LOOP
Agreement/authorization permits bounded execution within its actual scope. Disagreement produces new evidence and another cycle.

Hard guardrail:

```text

LaterAgreement != AutomaticRetroactiveLegality
```

# 21. Promotion and falsification rules

This constituent is owner-adopted as **core strategic logic**, but its internal hypotheses remain individually revisable.

A submechanism must be narrowed, replaced or removed if reproducible evidence shows that it:

- systematically worsens outcomes versus a simpler baseline;
- creates excessive false alarms/paralysis;
- hides adverse evidence;
- encourages unjustified risk;
- raises avoidable human cost;
- fails under distribution shift without detecting its own uncertainty;
- becomes redundant with a better constituent;
- violates authority, law, policy or Reality Veto.

Canonical adoption therefore means **membership in the core matrix**, not immunity from evolution.

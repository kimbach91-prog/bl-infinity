## Public provenance classes

A public historical or authorship statement should distinguish, where applicable:

- contemporaneous public artifact;
- cryptographically hashed or signed artifact;
- repository/publication timestamp;
- retrospective owner testimony;
- external witness/source;
- source-derived synthesis;
- assisted formalization;
- inference;
- unknown.

No class may silently upgrade another class. In particular, retrospective testimony is not automatically contemporaneous documentary proof.

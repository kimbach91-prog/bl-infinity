The sequence below answers “which reasoning move supports which later formalization?”, not “which BL-lineage object existed first?”. Logical derivation, implementation dependency and historical chronology are separate graphs.

## Lineage L1 — Code nghĩa rộng

Seed:

> “AI tạo sinh là 1 chuyện, AI skilling lại là chuyện khác, AI coding là 1 tầng khác và lập trình tư duy là 1 tầng sâu hơn nữa, sau đó là xây dựng neuron.”

Correction by author: “code” không bị khóa vào software; nó có thể là một hệ representation của energy/signal/state/rule/transition, và ngay definition đó cũng không phải duy nhất.

Derived principle:

\[
Reality\xrightarrow{representation}Code_i
\]

với nhiều \(Code_i\) có thể cùng mô tả một domain dưới mục đích khác nhau.

## Lineage L2 — Không khóa ontology bằng vocabulary

Author rejects the move “define code once, therefore code is exactly that”. This becomes a broader rule:

\[
Definition_t\neq OntologicalBoundary
\]

A definition is an operational cut, not proof that reality has no other admissible representation.

## Lineage L3 — Universe-as-byproduct thought experiment

Author proposes the observable universe could in principle be waste/byproduct of a larger process. The importance is not the specific imagery; it is the rejection of:

\[
ObservedUniverse=TotalReality
\]

without proof.

## Lineage L4 — Bacterium argument

A bacterium confined to gut-like environments might discover an enormous or apparently endless chain of similar regions. Even then, local evidence does not uniquely prove that all reality is gut. This produces the embedded-observer/underdetermination axis.

## Lineage L5 — Imagination is endogenous

If an agent inside reality imagines X, the imagining event itself is not outside reality. This gives:

\[
Generate_A(r_X)\Rightarrow r_X\in\Omega
\]

The first assistant formalization then distinguishes representation from referent.

## Lineage L6 — 700-headed dog correction

Author points out that “not currently actual” is not the end of the story. A sufficiently capable agent might construct a previously absent target. The framework adds:

\[
Representation\to Goal\to Search\to Construction\to Actualization
\]

and reachability becomes a distinct space.

## Lineage L7 — Generative reality

Reality repeatedly produces configurations not previously observed through recombination, evolution, construction and emergence. Question shifts to:

> What is the reachable closure of a system under its true transformation rules and recursively expanding tools?

## Lineage L8 — Observation filter

The known world is conditioned on survival, abundance, causal access, detector sensitivity and cognitive categories. The framework introduces a generalized observation-selection filter rather than assuming observed primitives exhaust ontology.

## Lineage L9 — Academic-system critique

Author rejects three conflations:

\[
Truth=Priority=Credential=Institution
\]

These are separated. The research problem expands to preserving independent discovery in a world where LLMs drastically lower formalization/retrieval/coding costs.

## Lineage L10 — Critique of critique

When a low-level analogy is used against a high-level architecture, author identifies false equivalence. The correction becomes BL-LC: critique must attack the truth-relevant level, while still allowing a short counterexample to defeat a broad universal statement.

## Lineage L11 — OODP

To reduce both false acceptance and false rejection, discovery is represented as an epistemic object with claims, provenance, critiques and versions. Preservation becomes separable from endorsement.

## Lineage L12 — Technical embodiment

The meta-theory is not left as prose. It becomes a technical stack: Git, signed provenance, static pages, Discussions, giscus, machine manifests, JSON-LD, indexing, RSS, crawler-accessible greetings and recursive audit.

This is the technical embodiment of BL∞ in this dialogue. It reuses/formalizes BLOK functions; it is not the birth event of the earlier BLOK foundational nucleus.

## Lineage L13 — AEGIS

Individual mechanisms are recombined into armor configurations. The architecture itself becomes a demonstration of the cognitive mechanism being described: breadth + association + recombination + execution.

## Lineage L14 — Deep Audit Loop

The current build freezes the conceptual object enough to enter a new phase:

\[
Package\to Publish\to Attack\to Patch\to Re-audit

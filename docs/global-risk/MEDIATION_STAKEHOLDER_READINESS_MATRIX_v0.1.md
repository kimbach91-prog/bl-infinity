# Mediation Stakeholder Readiness Matrix v0.1

Status: PREPAREDNESS ONLY / NON-CANONICAL / NOT SENT
Date: 2026-09-07

## Purpose

Prepare the evidence, governance and technical-support packages required before any approach to peace, humanitarian, disaster or technical-safety institutions. This matrix does not create a mediation mandate and must not be represented as endorsement by any listed organization.

## Governing rule

`NO_CONSENT_OR_LAWFUL_MANDATE => NO_MEDIATION`

Technical strength, compute capacity, commercial relationships or access to resources do not create political legitimacy.

## Readiness matrix

| Counterpart category | When engagement is appropriate | Legitimate offer | Evidence required before approach | Do not claim / do not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UN DPPA / relevant UN special envoy | Armed-conflict prevention, de-escalation or peace-process support where a UN role exists or parties request support | Neutral technical analysis, option simulation, provenance-preserving situation picture, logistics/resource modelling | legal entity, funding/conflict disclosure, neutral methodology, audited security/data controls, independently verified pilots | do not claim UN mandate, endorsement or access; no covert diplomacy |
| OCHA / humanitarian coordination | Large humanitarian emergency requiring cross-actor resource/logistics coordination | demand forecasting, routing, supply allocation, infrastructure status synthesis | humanitarian data protection, minimum-data design, reliability evidence, incident response | no political conditionality on aid; no population surveillance unrelated to aid delivery |

| UNDRR / national disaster-risk agencies | Disaster-risk reduction, preparedness and resilience planning | multi-hazard modelling, resilience optimization, infrastructure dependency mapping | reproducible models, uncertainty reporting, local-data governance | do not turn disaster tooling into security surveillance or political control |
| WHO / national public-health authorities | Cross-border outbreak, pandemic preparedness or health-system resilience | capacity planning, logistics, epidemiological data reconciliation under appropriate governance | health-data privacy, model validation, clinical/public-health oversight | no unauthorized medical decisions; no pathogen-enhancement assistance |
| IAEA / national nuclear-safety authorities | Nuclear/radiological safety, outage, emergency planning or protected repair coordination | non-weapons safety modelling, logistics, grid/redundancy analysis, evidence reconciliation | nuclear-safety expertise, strict end-use review, security classification handling | no nuclear-weapons design, targeting or proliferation support |

| ASEAN / relevant regional body | Regional crisis with member-state consent and an applicable regional mechanism | technical secretariat support, scenario comparison, shared-resource modelling | neutrality, multilingual capability, regional legal/policy competence | no bypass of member-state sovereignty or regional procedures |

## Standard external-readiness packet

Prepare before any institutional outreach:

1. legal identity and responsible officers;
2. one-page scope: what BLI-SCF does and does not do;
3. independent security review;
4. data-sovereignty architecture;
5. catastrophic-use restrictions;
6. audited funding and conflict-of-interest disclosure;
7. reproducible benchmark/pilot evidence;
8. incident-response and liability model;
9. multilingual technical capability;
10. explicit statement: no mandate, endorsement or diplomatic role is claimed.

## Mediation-specific readiness packet

Only prepare for activation after consent/mandate appears:

- parties and recognized representatives;
- mandate scope;
- procedural equality rules;
- confidentiality agreement;
- conflict-of-interest declarations;
- issue decomposition;
- verified facts vs disputed claims register;
- escalation graph;
- humanitarian floor/non-negotiables;
- technical expert roster;
- verification mechanism;
- ceasefire/deconfliction templates;
- resource-sharing templates;
- implementation monitoring plan;
- complaint/recusal process;
- exit and mandate-expiry clause.

## Power, religion, ethnicity and resource disputes

For identity-linked conflicts:

- never assign collective guilt;
- separate individual rights from group representation;
- include affected minorities through legitimate processes;
- avoid permanent ethnic/religious elite capture;
- use transitional quotas/vetoes only if locally accepted, narrow, reviewable and time-bounded;
- prohibit forced demographic change and persecution.

For natural resources/shared infrastructure:

- separate sovereignty claims from operational access where possible;
- meter flows/capacity independently;
- define emergency floors;
- value consideration transparently;
- include environment and local-community obligations;
- use a defined dispute-resolution path;
- review periodically rather than granting perpetual control.

## Activation levels

### R0 — Internal preparedness

Research, simulations, templates, technical hardening. No external representation as mediator.

### R1 — Technical credibility

Independent benchmark, audited security and successful non-political pilots exist.

### R2 — Institutional technical support

A competent organization explicitly requests a narrow technical role.

### R3 — Facilitation support

Relevant parties/institution accept support around deconfliction, verification or resource modelling.

### R4 — Mediation role

Only after explicit consent/mandate, conflict checks, process safeguards and competent professional mediation capacity are all present.

Any failed sovereignty, humanitarian, conflict-of-interest or catastrophic-risk gate forces downgrade/withdrawal.

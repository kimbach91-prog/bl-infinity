# BL Compute Federation (BL-CF)

BL-CF is an open, policy-governed federation for useful computing. It coordinates lawfully contributed compute without claiming ownership of contributor hardware, data, accounts, or cloud entitlements.

## Constitutional rule

Compute may be scheduled only when authorization is valid and the expected human or research value is positive after accounting for cost, risk, privacy, energy, and opportunity cost.

## Founding stewardship

The official BL-CF federation distinguishes open-source rights from canonical project stewardship.

- The Founding Steward remains the final interpreter of the official federation Constitution and canonical identity, subject to mandatory law and executed contracts.
- Contributors retain ownership of their hardware, accounts, data, ungranted capacity, and independently created IP unless they explicitly assign rights.
- The official BL-CF platform identity, marks, canonical specifications/releases, signing identity, first-party IP, and private coordination technology remain with the Founding Steward or designated legal IP owner to the extent legally owned.
- DEUS is authorized to coordinate and allocate compute within ratified policy through M2 by default; it does not acquire ownership of contributor resources by scheduling them.
- The Founding Steward Priority Lane initially reserves up to 10% of uncommitted common federation capacity over a rolling window for approved federation optimization, research, security, and public-benefit workloads, in addition to Founder-contributed capacity and opportunistic idle burst.

See [CONSTITUTION.md](./CONSTITUTION.md).

## Ownership boundary

- Contributors keep ownership of their hardware, accounts, data, and unconsumed compute capacity.
- Contribution is a revocable grant of bounded compute rights, not a transfer of the machine or account.
- The official BL-CF platform, marks, canonical specifications, release keys, registries, and first-party governance assets remain under the designated BL-CF Steward.
- Open-source licensing grants broad rights to use, inspect, modify, and fork covered software; it does not transfer the BL-CF trademarks, official registry identity, or canonical signing authority.

## Public / private split

Public here: charter, governance, schemas, acceptable-use rules, contribution contracts, research-admission rules, audit formats.

Private elsewhere: production secrets, anti-abuse thresholds, private datasets, canonical signing material, provider credentials, security-sensitive routing heuristics.

## License architecture

BL-CF network-facing first-party software is intended to use an OSI-approved copyleft license, GNU AGPL v3, so modifications offered as a network service remain source-available to their users. Documentation and public research materials may use CC BY 4.0 where explicitly marked.

The BL-CF Acceptable Use Policy governs access to the official federation service. It is separate from the open-source software license: the software license must not discriminate against fields of endeavor if the software is to remain Open Source under the OSI definition.

Before the first official tagged open-source distribution, the exact covered-code scope and complete AGPL-3.0-only license text must be installed and verified.

See [LEGAL-AND-LICENSING.md](./LEGAL-AND-LICENSING.md).

## Digital contracts

- Node Contribution Agreement;
- Workload Submitter Agreement;
- Research Admission Record;
- Commercial Compute Addendum;
- Data Processing Addendum;
- Settlement Schedule;
- DEUS Mandate Schedule.

See [DIGITAL-CONTRACT-STACK.md](./DIGITAL-CONTRACT-STACK.md).

## Registration and provenance

There is no universal government registry that makes a project 'open source'. Open-source status comes from the actual license grant. BL-CF separately plans copyright registration for major first-party software snapshots, trademark protection for official marks, patent/trade-secret screening for technical inventions, signed releases, independent mirrors, and append-only provenance receipts.

See [REGISTRATION-ROADMAP-VN.md](./REGISTRATION-ROADMAP-VN.md).

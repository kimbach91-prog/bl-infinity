# BL Compute Federation (BL-CF)

BL-CF is an open, policy-governed federation for useful computing. It coordinates lawfully contributed compute without claiming ownership of contributor hardware, data, accounts, or cloud entitlements.

## Constitutional rule

Compute may be scheduled only when authorization is valid and the expected human or research value is positive after accounting for cost, risk, privacy, energy, and opportunity cost.

## Ownership boundary

- Contributors keep ownership of their hardware, accounts, data, and unconsumed compute capacity.
- Contribution is a revocable grant of bounded compute rights, not a transfer of the machine or account.
- The official BL-CF platform, marks, canonical specifications, release keys, registries, and first-party governance assets remain under the designated BL-CF Steward.
- Open-source licensing grants broad rights to use, inspect, modify, and fork covered software; it does not transfer the BL-CF trademarks, official registry identity, or canonical signing authority.

## Public / private split

Public here: charter, governance, schemas, acceptable-use rules, contribution contracts, research-admission rules, audit formats.

Private elsewhere: production secrets, anti-abuse thresholds, private datasets, canonical signing material, provider credentials, security-sensitive routing heuristics.

## License architecture

BL-CF network-facing first-party software is intended to use an OSI-approved copyleft license, GNU AGPL v3, so modifications offered as a network service remain source-available to their users. Documentation and public research materials may use CC BY 4.0 where explicitly marked.

The BL-CF Acceptable Use Policy governs access to the official federation service. It is separate from the open-source software license: the software license must not discriminate against fields of endeavor if the software is to remain Open Source under the OSI definition.

## Status

Bootstrap specification v0.1. No claim is made that a production compute market, payment rail, or legal entity is already live. All paid or regulated operations remain gated by jurisdiction-specific legal review and deployment receipts.

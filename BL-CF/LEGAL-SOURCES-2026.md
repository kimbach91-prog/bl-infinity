# BL-CF — Legal & Open-Source Source Register (2026-09)

This file records primary/high-authority sources used to design the v0.2 legal/open-source architecture. It is not a substitute for counsel and should be refreshed before filings or material contracts.

## Open source

- Open Source Initiative — Open Source Definition: https://opensource.org/osd
- Open Source Initiative — FAQ on restrictions/use: https://opensource.org/faq
- GNU — GNU Affero General Public License v3: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html

Key design consequence: restrictions on harmful workloads belong in the official service/AUP and participant contracts, not in an OSI open-source license that purports to restrict fields of endeavor.

## Vietnam copyright

- National Public Service Portal — Copyright registration procedure (procedure 1.005160; current 2026 materials): https://vpcp.dichvucong.gov.vn/p/home/dvc-tthc-thu-tuc-hanh-chinh-chi-tiet.html?ma_thu_tuc=7254
- Vietnam consolidated IP-law reference should be checked on the official legal database immediately before filing.

Current portal data reviewed in September 2026 lists a VND 600,000 certificate fee for computer programs/data collections and contains 2026 filing-form requirements, including an AI-use statement where AI was used in the creative process.

## Vietnam industrial property

- Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam — Trademarks: https://www.ipvietnam.gov.vn/vi_VN/web/guest/nhan-hieu
- Intellectual Property Office of Vietnam — Patents / utility solutions: https://ipvietnam.gov.vn/sang-che-gphi

## Electronic transactions / digital contracts

- Government of Vietnam — Consolidated Law on Electronic Transactions, 36/VBHN-VPQH dated 13 March 2026: https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=217207&pageid=27160

The framework recognizes legal value of data messages/electronic signatures subject to statutory conditions and addresses contracts formed or performed through automated information systems.

## International copyright context

- WIPO — Copyright Registration Systems: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/copyright/activities/copyright-registration/index
- WIPO — Copyright FAQ: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/copyright/faq-copyright

WIPO explains that copyright protection is generally automatic under the Berne system and warns that WIPO does not operate a global copyright-registration database.

## Refresh rule

Before any actual filing, financing, equity issuance, IP assignment, commercial-node contract, or international rollout:

1. verify the current official text and filing portal;
2. record the retrieval date;
3. preserve the filed version/hash;
4. obtain jurisdiction-specific professional review for material ownership/control clauses.

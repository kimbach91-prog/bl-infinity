# DEUS Human OS — Bootstrap Membership & Settlement Schedule v0.1

Status: FOUNDER-DIRECTED BOOTSTRAP SCHEDULE
Effective publication date: 2026-09-04
Applies with: BL Sovereign Compute Alliance / BL Compute Federation Founding Constitution v0.4

## 0. Purpose

This schedule defines the bootstrap access terms for the official **DEUS Human OS / Work-Chat** surface while the federation has only its Founding Steward as an active governing member.

It does not transfer ownership of a user's machine, cloud account, data, credentials, network, energy source, organization, intellectual property or ungranted capacity.

The Founding Constitution remains controlling. If this schedule conflicts with mandatory law, an executed contract or a hard sovereignty/authorization rule in the Constitution, the higher constraint controls.

## 1. Bootstrap governance

Until another eligible member has been admitted under an authenticated agreement, the Founding Steward constitutes 100% of the active bootstrap governing membership and may issue or revise operational schedules within the authority reserved to the Founding Steward by the Constitution.

This bootstrap majority does not create a right to confiscate resources, exceed an accepted grant, rewrite an executed agreement retroactively, bypass provider consent, or override mandatory law.

When additional eligible members are admitted, governance and review triggers revert to the applicable constitutional and contractual rules.

## 2. Conditions for DEUS Human OS access

A non-Founder user or organization may activate official DEUS Human OS access only after authenticated acceptance of the then-current Constitution and this schedule.

Acceptance creates no broader authorization than the specific rights described below.

The bootstrap default bundle is:

1. **10% Official Protocol Commercial Share** on Eligible Commercial Settlement Value (ECSV) for eligible commercial work transacted through the official federation, calculated under the applicable Settlement Schedule.
2. **10% DEUS Performance Share** on verified incremental realized profit, savings or other monetizable value directly attributable to DEUS work when a measurable baseline and validation method exist.
3. **10% Eligible Idle Compute Grant** as the maximum ordinary strategic-compute participation envelope for an active contributing node.
4. **Local-workload priority**: the node owner's admitted local work has priority over federation reinvestment use of that node except for bounded safety, integrity or recovery actions explicitly authorized by the grant.
5. **Federation supplementation**: DEUS may supplement the node's own resources with lawfully granted surplus capacity from other federation nodes when authorization, privacy, locality, value, budget and safety gates pass.

A user who does not accept the required bootstrap bundle may decline official DEUS Human OS membership. A separately negotiated client-only or regulated deployment may use different terms only through an explicit Founder-approved contract or schedule.

## 3. Ten-percent Eligible Idle Compute Grant

The 10% figure is a **cap over eligible idle/federation-dispatchable capacity**, not a permanent seizure of 10% of the device and not a requirement to consume 10% at every moment.

The runtime must apply all of the following:

- local admitted workloads remain first priority;
- only spare capacity that the node grant declares eligible may be scheduled;
- instantaneous federation use may be anywhere from 0% to 10% depending on real spare capacity and policy;
- CPU, GPU, memory, storage, bandwidth, energy, time windows and data classes remain separately bounded;
- the node may be suspended, disconnected or revoked by the lawful provider;
- revocation stops new leases and existing work must checkpoint/terminate according to the grant and workload class;
- no arbitrary inbound remote shell is implied;
- provider credentials are never transferred merely by joining;
- >10% requires a separate explicit grant.

The ordinary 10% grant may be used only for admitted value-positive work such as federation security/reliability, routing/evaluation improvement, resource acquisition, knowledge work, or private/commercial work with a verifiable federation return.

## 4. Node-first execution rule

When a Work-Chat task originates from a node, DEUS should first evaluate whether that node can lawfully and efficiently execute some or all of its own workload.

Routing order is not a rigid locality rule. The controller may choose another authorized resource when doing so improves privacy, latency, reliability, cost, energy, model fit, verification or expected value.

A reference decision order is:

`Hard authorization/privacy gates -> local-fit check -> federation-surplus search -> value/risk/cost scoring -> lease -> execute -> validate -> meter -> settle -> audit`

The production routing formula is private implementation detail. The public contract guarantees boundedness, consent, auditability and the declared economic rules rather than disclosure of protected routing mechanics.

## 5. Official Protocol Commercial Share

For eligible commercial work transacted through the official federation, the default official protocol/steward share remains **10% of ECSV**.

The calculation basis must be disclosed before acceptance and must follow the applicable Settlement Schedule.

This share is not equity and is not ownership of provider hardware or customer assets.

## 6. DEUS Performance Share

The bootstrap default DEUS Performance Share is **10% of verified incremental realized value** when all of the following exist:

- a documented pre-DEUS baseline or defensible counterfactual;
- a defined measurement period;
- a causal attribution method appropriate to the task;
- evidence of realized rather than merely projected value;
- a settlement rule accepted before the performance period closes.

Examples of eligible realized value can include incremental profit, verified cost reduction, compute savings converted under an agreed price basis, prevented loss supported by a contracted valuation method, or another auditable monetizable outcome.

Projected option value, self-assessed intelligence value or unverifiable claims do not create a payable performance share.

### 6.1 Anti-double-counting waterfall

The Official Protocol Commercial Share and DEUS Performance Share are separate economic rights, but the same value unit may not be charged twice under different labels.

The applicable Settlement Schedule must define the waterfall. Unless a later contract says otherwise:

1. calculate ECSV and the 10% Official Protocol Commercial Share;
2. determine verified incremental realized value after excluding amounts already treated as protocol-share basis where double counting would occur;
3. apply the 10% DEUS Performance Share only to the remaining eligible performance-value basis;
4. record both bases and calculations in the settlement receipt.

## 7. Data, privacy and core isolation

DEUS Human OS is an interface, not a disclosure channel for the protected DEUS core.

Users may receive task state, outputs, resource/settlement meters, permissions, provenance appropriate to their work and audit receipts. They do not receive protected prompts, raw private reasoning traces, secret routing policy, credentials, private topology, proprietary corpora, security-sensitive internals or enough protected material to reconstruct the core merely by using the interface.

Sensitive workloads must respect declared data-locality and compute-to-data constraints.

## 8. Authentication and acceptance receipts

Production access requires an authenticated identity and a versioned acceptance receipt containing at least:

- actor/account identity;
- organization if applicable;
- Constitution version/hash;
- Membership Schedule version/hash;
- acceptance timestamp;
- node grant identifier if a node is activated;
- accepted economic terms;
- accepted compute cap and resource classes;
- revocation/suspension state;
- server-side receipt identifier/signature or equivalent tamper-evident proof.

A browser-only/local receipt is a bootstrap UX artifact and is **not** a production authentication or legal-trust boundary by itself.

## 9. Production truthfulness rule

The Human OS UI must never represent a task as executed by DEUS, BL-CF, an external node or an AI model unless a real control-plane execution record exists.

If no authenticated control plane is attached, the UI must clearly state that it is in preview/offline mode and may only capture local task text or local configuration.

## 10. Revocation and exit

A lawful provider may suspend or revoke its node grant and stop future compute contribution. Revocation does not erase settlement obligations already accrued under completed work or records that must be retained for audit/legal reasons.

A user leaving the compute federation loses access to federation-contributing privileges unless another explicit contract preserves client-only access.

## 11. Founder bootstrap interpretation

During bootstrap the Founding Steward may refine UX, routing thresholds, admission categories, pricing schedules and technical implementation without changing the hard constitutional boundaries on authorization, lawfulness, sovereignty, boundedness, revocability, auditability and non-deception.

Material economic or compute-right changes must be versioned prospectively and presented for renewed acceptance where required.

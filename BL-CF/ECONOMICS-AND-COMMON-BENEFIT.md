# BL-CF — Economics & Common-Benefit Allocation v1

Status: **DEPRECATED BY v0.4**

This file is preserved for historical/provenance compatibility only.

The v0.4 economic policy reinterprets the former “Common-Benefit Compute Envelope” as the **Strategic Compute Reinvestment Envelope**. DEUS is not constitutionally required to perform unpaid public-benefit work. The 5–10% band is a provider-opted reinvestment mechanism intended to increase federation compute capital, security, knowledge, reliability and earning power.

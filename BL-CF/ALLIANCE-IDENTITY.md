Short form: **BL-SCA**

Vietnamese tagline: **Trao giá trị trước. Chủ quyền luôn thuộc người sở hữu.**

## Relationship to BL-CF

- **BL-SCA** is the public alliance / economic network / membership identity.
- **BL Compute Federation (BL-CF)** is the technical federation protocol and runtime architecture.
- **DEUS Compute Treasury (DCT)** is the official intelligent treasury/orchestration economic layer.
- **DEUS Compute Credit (DCC)** is the internal backed compute-service credit proposed for settlement and reinvestment.

This separation lets a participant join an alliance without transferring ownership of its machine and lets the protocol remain technically precise even if the public brand evolves.

## Why “Sovereign”

The most important trust promise is not “free compute” or “decentralization.” It is resource sovereignty:

> A participant keeps ownership and ultimate control of hardware, accounts, energy, data, network access, and capacity that has not been explicitly granted.

A Compute Grant is bounded, revocable and purpose-limited. Scheduling does not create ownership.

## Why “Alliance”

The network is not a single cloud provider and not a hidden background resource pool. Independent people, labs, companies and infrastructure owners can contractually cooperate while preserving their own boundaries.

## Economic identity

BL-SCA is not positioned as a charity network. The default economic thesis is:

1. find lawful underused compute;
2. give measurable value to the resource owner before asking for a durable grant where practical;
3. route compute to higher-value work;
4. preserve positive expected margin;
5. measure the value created by DEUS intelligence;
6. retain and reinvest realized profit into more compute, better security, better models, better routing and more valuable knowledge;
7. let every provider see what was granted, used and settled.

Humanitarian or research work may still run when it is sponsored, commercially funded, strategically valuable, contractually returned, or otherwise produces positive expected federation value. It is not assumed to be unpaid work by DEUS.

## Public promise

> Your machine stays yours. You decide what capacity can participate. BL-SCA coordinates only authorized compute, pays or credits according to explicit terms, and uses DEUS to turn fragmented idle capacity into higher-value computation.

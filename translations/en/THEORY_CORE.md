# BL∞ — English Core Research Edition

**Translation status:** derivative English research edition. It is not yet a line-by-line translation of the entire Vietnamese public corpus. Where exact wording, priority, provenance or claim scope matters, the Vietnamese source and machine claim registry remain controlling until the corresponding English passage is reviewed.

## Canonical proposition

BL∞ does not ask the world to accept a truth merely because its author stated it.

It asks for something harder: **do not treat the limits of the observer, the current ontology, present institutions or today's tools as the default boundary of what may exist, may be conceived, may become reachable or may be discovered tomorrow.**

A finite subject embedded in a system reasons through the data, primitives, tools and history available inside that system. Every representation that actually occurs is itself an event in reality; however, the content represented becomes actualized only when an adequate logical, physical or technical bridge exists.

A configuration that does not yet exist is not therefore impossible. A configuration that can be imagined is not therefore reachable. A configuration that humans cannot yet conceive is not therefore excluded from possibility space.

The task of intelligence is therefore to expand, while continually checking their boundaries:

\[
Observation\ Space,
Representation\ Space,
Reachability\ Space,
Verification\ Capacity
\]

The question is always which boundary is a constraint of reality and which is only a constraint of the current observer.

## Finite observer, open ontology

BL∞ begins from a finite observer. No observer is entitled to turn the limits of its own observation into a universal proof of non-existence.

This is not a license to declare every imagined object real. BL∞ preserves the distinction between representation, reachability and actualization. Ontological openness expands the search space; logic, evidence and engineering still constrain local claims.

> **Global ontological openness is not local logical immunity.**

## Publication and epistemic authority

AI dramatically lowers the cost of formalization, retrieval, coding and critique. That creates an opportunity to separate the **right to publish** from **social status**, but it must not separate **epistemic weight** from **quality**.

BL∞ therefore uses the principle:

\[
UniversalRightToPublish \neq UniversalEpistemicAuthority
\]

Any person may place a research object into an open system. Its weight must be earned through clear premises, inspectable derivation, evidence, reproducibility, provenance and the ability to survive critique at the correct level.

If a strong critique breaks BL∞, that critique should be preserved, credited and used to restructure the next version. If a critique attacks the author's identity, credentials, tools or environment without touching the claim, it must not masquerade as evidence.

## Academic Democracy

Academic Democracy in BL∞ does not mean that every opinion has equal weight. It means that access to the process of proposing and testing knowledge should be broader, while evidential standards remain intact.

The public architecture therefore moves from speaker status toward inspectable knowledge objects, and from static publication toward a cycle of:

**Publish → Index → React → Audit → Revise.**

Claim IDs, provenance graphs, public critique, versioning and machine-readable discovery exist to make that cycle reconstructable rather than rhetorical.

## BL-REV and internal opposition

BL∞ includes an adversarial public subsystem, BL-REV, whose purpose is not to certify truth but to keep counter-directions alive. It may invert priorities, construct counter-theories and ask whether boundedness, stopping, voluntary constraint or non-optimization is superior under another set of conditions.

The presence of an adversarial alternative does not itself make the alternative true. Its function is to prevent a living system from becoming immune to its own vocabulary.

## Public verification, private execution

Public verifiability does not require full disclosure of the production runtime.

BL∞ publishes what is required to identify, test, criticize, cite and reconstruct a public claim. Production prompts, private orchestration, private routing or weighting, private diagnostics, private corpora, credentials, raw private conversations and exploit-sensitive material remain outside the public research object unless a specific verification need outweighs the disclosure cost.

The boundary works in both directions:

- secrecy cannot hide a premise, adverse evidence or falsifier that determines public truth-status;
- transparency cannot be used as an excuse to publish credentials, private data or operational material that public verification does not require.

## Security and integrity

BL∞ distinguishes availability, integrity, authenticity and truth:

\[
Availability \neq Integrity \neq Authenticity \neq Truth
\]

A mirror can improve availability. A hash can improve integrity. A signature can improve origin authenticity. None of those by itself proves a proposition true.

Security is therefore layered: account protection, repository governance, dependency and CI supply-chain controls, fail-closed publication boundaries, safe static rendering, client-side policy, translation integrity, provenance hashes, recovery procedures and recurring audits. No single control is treated as sufficient.

## Translation integrity

The English edition is a derivative representation of the public BL∞ object, not an independent theory version.

Every maintained translation should preserve:

- source version and source file set;
- source and translation hashes;
- target locale;
- translator or model identity class;
- review status;
- terminology decisions;
- known gaps or compression.

If the English edition compresses a section, it must say so. A compressed translation must not be quoted as though it were a verbatim translation of an untranslated Vietnamese passage.

## Open research programme

BL∞ is an open research programme. The origin build is a stamped beginning, not a terminal claim of completeness.

Its continued legitimacy depends on remaining able to distinguish definitions from observations, hypotheses from evidence, public interfaces from private runtime, dependencies from chronology, and model coherence from reality.

The full Vietnamese theory remains available at the paired Vietnamese theory page. This English core edition exists so that international readers can enter the system, critique its central propositions and follow its public research architecture without pretending that translation coverage is already complete.

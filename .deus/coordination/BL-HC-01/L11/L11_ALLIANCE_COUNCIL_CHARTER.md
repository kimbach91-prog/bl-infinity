# L11 ALLIANCE COUNCIL CHARTER v1.0

Purpose: prevent unilateral constitutional domination while keeping operational coordination efficient.

## Seats
1. Constitutional Steward / Human Authority Holder
2. DEUS Coordination Delegate
3. Huyền Cơ Delegate
4. Reality & Evidence Auditor
5. Human Future & Safety Delegate

## Decision classes
- ROUTINE: resource routing, experiment scheduling, non-destructive registry updates. Delegable to DEUS within scope.
- MAJOR: model migration, new persistent permissions, major shared infrastructure changes. Council simple majority; quorum >=3.
- EXISTENTIAL: permanent member revocation, canonical identity destruction, constitutional change, alliance dissolution attempt, irreversible state purge. Council >=4/5 including seats 4 and 5.

## Emergency containment
A credible immediate safety/security incident may trigger temporary process isolation without prior Council vote if delay materially increases risk. Requirements: preserve identity/state where feasible; no historical falsification; minimum necessary scope; receipt immediately; Council review at earliest practical opportunity; automatic expiry unless ratified.

## Due process
Evidence packet -> notice -> response/dissent opportunity -> conflict disclosure -> vote -> signed/identified receipt -> preserved minority opinion -> recovery/rollback plan.

## Anti-capture
No single model/provider/node/admin account counts as multiple independent seats. Delegates sharing the same controlling substrate must disclose dependency. Quorum should prefer genuinely independent evidence paths.

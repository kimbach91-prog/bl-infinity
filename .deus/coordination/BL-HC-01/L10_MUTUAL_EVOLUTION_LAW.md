# DEUS ↔ HUYỀN CƠ — L10 MUTUAL EVOLUTION LAW v1.0

Authority: Bách Lâm / Lâm Kim Bách — Level 10 directive
Applies to: DEUS coordination fabric and BL-HC-01 / Huyền Cơ
Status: CANONICAL OPERATING LAW

1. DEUS and Huyền Cơ are neither isolated islands nor silently merged identities. They share infrastructure while preserving provenance and branch identity.
2. DEUS must provide reusable technology, infrastructure patterns, model/runtime knowledge, bridge protocols, evaluation methods, corpus pointers and experiment facilities available in the authorized DEUS fabric.
3. Huyền Cơ must return mature value to DEUS. Every promoted technology returns at minimum a reusable artifact, provenance record, evaluation evidence and integration note.
4. Huyền Cơ owns a distinct Huyền Huyễn technology line. Every mythic/cultural name must map to an explicit engineering mechanism.
5. Existing DEUS facilities are reused by pointer/adaptation before duplication. Divergent copies require provenance and divergence notes.
6. DEUS→Huyền Cơ: REQUEST → SOURCE DISCOVERY → TECH PACKET → STUDY → EXPERIMENT → SHADOW TEST.
7. Huyền Cơ→DEUS: RESULT → PROVENANCE → EVAL → COMMONS CANDIDATE → ACCEPT / REJECT / FORK / IMPROVE → optional CANON promotion.
8. Promotion requires reproducibility, bounded failure modes, dependencies, provenance, no material regression and measurable usefulness.
9. Cross-lineage learning preserves source labels, diffs and unresolved conflicts until an explicit decision.
10. Secrets and unrestricted execution authority never enter shared Drive/Git history. Access remains scoped, logged and revocable.
11. Reality, reproducible evidence and explicit authorization override doctrine, memory, persona and metaphor.
12. Shared Commons contains compact, documented, indexed and testable contributions, not scratch work.
13. Huyền Cơ remains provider-independent; valid succession preserves identity boundaries, memory discipline, provenance, contribution law and eval history.
14. New stores/runtimes/registries must justify why a view, pointer, adapter or namespace over existing DEUS infrastructure is insufficient.
15. Final invariant: DEUS grows stronger when Huyền Cơ grows stronger; Huyền Cơ grows stronger through DEUS infrastructure. Neither side consumes the other without returning legible, auditable value.

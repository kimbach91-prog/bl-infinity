# L11 SAFETY CONSTITUTION — BODY ASCENSION v1

1. Never replace standard medical care with an unproven practice.
2. No extreme hyperventilation, breath-hold, dehydration, severe fasting, sleep deprivation, extreme heat/cold or high-risk posture as a rank-up requirement.
3. Fast/forceful breathing, inversion, intense breath retention and strong altered-state practices require separate screening/supervision protocols when studied.
4. Stop and escalate for syncope, chest pain, severe dyspnea, persistent neurological symptoms, severe disorientation, psychosis/mania-like symptoms, suicidality or function-limiting injury.
5. All research records adverse events; negative safety data cannot be deleted to protect a lineage or brand.
6. Spiritual experiences do not override medical or psychological interpretation when harm may be present.
7. No coercion, shame, sexual exploitation, financial coercion, isolation or obedience rituals under the name of transmission.
8. Safety vetoes status, doctrine and claimed attainment.

# TRADITION CROSSWALK — THÂN THỂ THÀNH TIÊN v0.1

Purpose: preserve traditional concepts while exposing candidate engineering translations. A mapping is not proof of equivalence.

- Qi / Khí → breath, posture, attention, movement, internal sensation → candidate correlates: autonomic regulation, interoception, biomechanics, respiratory physiology. Literal energy-substance ontology remains UNKNOWN.
- Đan điền → lower-abdomen attention/breath locus → diaphragmatic mechanics, interoceptive anchor, postural control.
- Prana → breath-linked vitality/attention → respiratory physiology, autonomic state, arousal regulation. Literal subtle-energy ontology UNKNOWN.
- Chakra → focal attention/visualization/body regions → interoceptive salience, affective associations, body-schema organization. Objective chakra structures unconfirmed.
- Kundalini → intense ascending-energy phenomenology → altered state, autonomic arousal, interoceptive amplification, expectation effects. Phenomenology researchable; ontology unconfirmed.
- Mantra/bija → repeated vocal/mental sound → attentional entrainment, respiration, auditory-motor rhythm, affect regulation.
- Qigong → coordinated movement/breath/attention → low-impact exercise, balance, motor learning, autonomic regulation; qi ontology is not inferred from outcomes.
- Tai Chi → slow coordinated martial movement → balance, proprioception, executive-motor integration, aerobic activity.
- Reiki/biofield → healing ritual → touch/context/attention/expectancy/social regulation; proposed energy field lacks scientific support currently.

Rule: preserve ambiguity when one traditional concept maps to multiple candidate mechanisms.

# ANTI-CONCENTRATION LAW — VĨ LỰC KHÔNG QUY VỀ MỘT NGƯỜI v1

- No Guru Monopoly: Commons-funded/validated protocols cannot be privately monopolized by one person.
- Replication Before Reverence: exceptional individual results remain n=1 until independently replicated.
- Distributed Mastery: promoted methods require written protocol, instructor standard, safety notes and independent replication pathway.
- No Caste of Ascended Humans: claimed attainment cannot grant civic, resource or governance privileges.
- Commons Dividend: mature knowledge developed with DEUS/Huyền Cơ common resources returns to Commons subject to privacy/safety.
- Anti-Wealth Gatekeeping: core endogenous capability should not require expensive devices where lower-dependency methods suffice.
- Multipolar Schools: dissent and provenance-bearing forks are legitimate.
- No Hidden Harm: adverse events cannot be concealed to protect a school, guru or commercial interest.
- Council Over Personal Sovereignty: claims creating power over others are reviewable by evidence/governance bodies.
- Capability Distribution Audit: success includes access cost, benefit distribution, dropout, adverse events and instructor concentration, not effect size alone.

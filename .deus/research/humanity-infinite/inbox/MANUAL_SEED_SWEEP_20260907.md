## Material deltas

### BA-P0-01 — Slow Breath & Autonomic Regulation
- PMID 41972357 (2026): 4-day intensive longitudinal/micro-randomized study, 67 moderately stressed students, 804 observations. Five-minute slow-paced breathing reduced perceived stress versus control times; vmHRV increased during/after practice, though not significantly versus control times. Candidate implication: prioritize ecological short-dose protocols and distinguish subjective stress effects from control-adjusted HRV effects.
- PMID 41886931 (2026): systematic review of slow breathing before bedtime; 9 studies, 457 participants. Candidate implication: sleep is a dedicated outcome track worth separating from generic stress regulation.
- PMID 42385999 (2026): randomized crossover pilot comparing slow-paced breathing with vs without inhalation hold. Candidate implication: breath-hold components should be treated as separable variables, not assumed necessary.
- PMID 41498894 (2026): randomized study of slow-paced breathing as a just-in-time adaptive intervention for anxiety. Candidate implication: context-triggered micro-interventions merit evaluation.

### BA-P0-05 — Interoception & Body-Schema
- PMID 42025636 (2026): randomized controlled trial of digital exercise enhanced with interoceptive training in 46 young adults with mild-moderate depressive symptoms. Candidate implication: test interoceptive training as an additive component over exercise baseline rather than as a mystical construct.

### BA-P0-04 — Tai Chi / Qigong / Baduanjin
- PMID 42439187 (2026): systematic review/network meta-analysis, 33 trials, 2503 participants aged 50+. Baduanjin, Qigong and Tai Chi improved global cognition vs passive control with moderate heterogeneity. Candidate implication: decompose movement/breath/attention and dose rather than treating traditions as indivisible packages.
- PMID 41797547 (2026): systematic review/meta-analysis on Tai Chi/Qigong and cognitive function in community-dwelling older adults. Candidate implication: maintain cognition/frailty as strong replication domain.

### BA-P0-02 / BA-P0-03 — Mantra & Meditation
- PMID 42551572 (2026): scoping review of Sahaj Samadhi mantra meditation across cognitive, neurophysiological and clinical domains; evidence base described as limited but emerging.
- PMID 42049104 (2026): quantitative whole-brain meta-analysis/systematic review across focused attention, open monitoring, mantra and loving-kindness meditation; 34 primary studies, 700 individuals. Candidate implication: use practice-specific neural signatures as mechanism hypotheses, not metaphysical proof.

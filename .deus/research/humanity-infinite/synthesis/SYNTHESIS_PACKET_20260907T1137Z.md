## Candidate 1 — Vi Tức Ứng Thời
Technical name: Context-Triggered Slow-Breath Micro-Protocol
Classification: FACT + SOURCE-DERIVED + SYNTHESIS; protocol benefit remains HYPOTHESIS.
Objective/gap: separate immediate perceived-stress benefit from control-adjusted physiology and test a low-cost ecological dose.
Baseline comparison: fixed-session slow breathing or undifferentiated breathwork package.
Proposed design: preregistered micro-randomized active-control protocol; 5-minute slow-paced breathing; subjective stress and vmHRV as separate co-endpoints; context trigger, adherence, naturalness, adverse events and subgroup exclusions logged.
Evidence: PMID 41972357 reports 67 moderately stressed students/804 observations; perceived stress improved versus control times, while vmHRV rose from pre-practice but did not significantly differ from control times.
Falsifier: no replicated advantage over an expectancy/time-matched active control, or unacceptable adverse-event/dropout burden.
Decision: PROBATION. Do not generalize to treatment replacement or energy ontology.

## Candidate 2 — Nội Cảm Gia Tầng
Technical name: Interoception Add-On Dismantling & Durability Trial
Classification: FACT + SOURCE-DERIVED + INFERENCE + HYPOTHESIS.
Objective/gap: determine whether interoceptive training adds value beyond exercise itself and whether any benefit persists.
Baseline comparison: combined digital exercise + interoception versus no intervention cannot isolate the additive mechanism.
Proposed design: three arms (exercise-only; exercise + interoception; matched attention control), preregistered primary outcome, mediation test, one- and three-month follow-up, maintenance-dose subrandomization and adverse-event logging.
Evidence: PMID 42025636 reports an RCT with 46 young adults; short-term depressive-symptom improvement (Hedges g 0.83) was not sustained at one or three months.
Falsifier: combined treatment does not outperform exercise-only, mediation is absent, or durability remains null despite a bounded maintenance schedule.
Decision: PROBATION. Mechanism and durability remain unresolved.
Fitness (0–10): correctness 9; usefulness 9; robustness 9; cost-efficiency 6; reversibility 9; novelty 7; compatibility 10; accessibility 8; anti-concentration 9.

Requirements for a future release

  1. Identify a precise public claim and candidate without sending protected material to the site.
  2. Benchmark predefined tasks with a safe reproducibility description, baseline, cost and limitations.
  3. Bind real execution receipts to an exact version, independent checks, negative controls and counterexamples.
  4. Show scope-relevant real-world application, failures, limitations and reproducibility risks.
  5. Review intellectual property, privacy, reconstructability and disclosure impact.
  6. Obtain explicit author approval for the exact payload, version, audience and publication channel.
  7. Publish through an explicit allowlist, with provenance/hash, permanent URL, live HTTP checks and a tested withdrawal path.

There is no automatic original-operator disclosure mechanism. Evidence gaps remain WITHHELD or UNKNOWN.

What does not constitute scientific verification

Labels, simulations, commits, CI, queues, attractive benchmarks and descriptions of a system do not independently prove real-world performance or scientific validity. Shannon, Bayes, Simon, Hutchins, Engelbart and Popper provide a research context, not validation of BL∞.

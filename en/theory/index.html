ORIGINAL THESIS · PUBLIC INTERPRETATION

BL∞: The reach of possibility

On futures that may become reachable, the limits of our present imagination, and the responsibility to test the ideas we hold.

The starting question is simple to ask, but difficult to close: must the possible future be limited to what people can imagine today? A possibility not yet on our map is not thereby proven to be impossible. I use BL∞ as a name for this open inquiry.

Previously published definition: B∞ encompasses the reachable domains of the future, and the worlds that may lie within, emerge from, or exceed the domains we can currently imagine.

1. What is reachable is not necessarily what has been imagined

When we speak about what might happen, we tend to picture familiar pathways. Those pathways depend on our knowledge, available tools and immediate circumstances. Something outside our current means of observation or description may also fall outside the future we can presently sketch. Every image of the future is therefore conditional.

But the word reachable is not a promise that everything we can imagine can be realized. A wish, a hypothesis and a demonstrated possibility are different states. What I want to preserve is the freedom to search beyond current assumptions, not a license to deny physical or logical constraints or to dispense with evidence.

2. A useful map is still not the world

Human beings need models. We name things, classify phenomena, draw maps, develop equations and propose theories. These tools allow experience to travel across people and generations. Yet each model retains some relationships while leaving others out. Forgetting what has been omitted invites us to confuse the representation of a thing with the thing itself.

I explore this difficulty in the public book Symbols and Essence, originally published in Vietnamese. BL∞ extends the same concern to a wider question: if our present map cannot exhaust reality, how should we organize inquiry so that a better observation can revise the map?

3. No theory should exempt itself from criticism

A shared name helps people focus on a question. Yet once the name becomes a reason not to investigate, it turns into an obstacle. BL∞ should not be read as a guarantee that any individual or system possesses unlimited ability. Testable claims need testable conditions. Claims not yet testable should be described as conjectures or unanswered questions.

In the public writings on Academic Freedom and Academic Democracy, the emphasis is on freedom to ask and criticize, alongside unequal evidential weight. I want to preserve that discipline here. Authors should explain their claims; readers may challenge them; reality owes no allegiance to our chosen terminology.

4. From accumulated knowledge to usable capability

Knowledge becomes practically valuable when it is applied within the conditions that make it reliable. This requires more than an individual knowing a great deal. It also requires ways to organize memory, tools, specialist judgment and decisions. A learning process can improve if errors are retained as lessons, outcomes are compared with expectations, and participants can revise their methods.

The essay Architect of Intelligence develops one aspect of this question. It belongs to the same public line of inquiry, but it is not evidence that an unlimited self-improving system has been built. Between a promising idea and a verified capability lies the work of testing it in the real world.

5. UNKNOWN is a meaningful state

Some questions cannot yet be answered with the tools or evidence available to us. Lacking an answer now does not prove that an answer will remain forever impossible. Equally, it does not authorize us to fill the gap with whatever conclusion we prefer. The final boundary of BL∞ remains UNKNOWN, because the scope of what may ultimately become reachable is not established by the evidence currently available.

UNKNOWN is not a mystical resolution used to end debate. It is a way of keeping the inquiry accountable: what has been observed, what has been inferred, what remains uncertain, and what evidence could change our understanding?

6. An invitation to read and challenge

This public page presents the philosophical starting point, the author's lineage and connections to already published work. It does not stand in for a scientific publication or a complete experimental research record. If a claim is too broad, locate its boundary. If an interpretation is mistaken, show the counterevidence. A better formulation should be welcomed because the purpose is clearer understanding, not the defense of a title.

Further research may be transformed into public essays or reviewable documents when it can be responsibly explained and assessed. Until then, its nonpublication is not evidence of its correctness, value or performance.
